Shortcuts

Sonus is designed to be used with a microphone in one hand and a mouse in the other. These are the keys worth knowing.

Global keys

ActionShortcut
Start or stop live audioSpace
Record a takeR
Choose a processing stage1 – 5
Open the preset libraryClick the preset name
Save the current sound as a presetCtrl+S
Close a sheet or popoverEsc

Stage numbers

KeyStage
1Clean — gate and room floor.
2Tone — EQ, low cut and high cut.
3Dynamics — compressor, lift and limiter.
4Voice — pitch, vocal size, resonance, brightness and texture.
5Space — room, mix, echo and echo time.

Pointer controls

WhereWhat to do
Tone graphDrag Low, Body, Presence or Air. Horizontal changes frequency; vertical changes gain.
Tone cut handlesUse the Low cut and High cut sliders below the graph.
Dynamics graphDrag the knee handle for threshold/lift; drag the top handle for ratio.
Clean graphDrag the threshold node left/right; drag vertically for reduction.
Voice graphDrag horizontally to add harmonic texture.
Device pickersClick anywhere on a picker to open the dark device list.
TakesUse the play and RAW buttons; use the trash button to discard both files.
Typing disables shortcuts. While a text field or a sheet is focused, the global keys step aside so typing and names behave normally.
